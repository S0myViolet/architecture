Sandbox notes

This is a living sandbox, not a finished 3D model. Scenes come from real video frames; the “intended look” comes from the design PDF. The garden excavation (possible pool) and the upper terraces have no design pages yet — those areas show the site only.

How to update later

  • data/rooms.json — room names, notes, frame & reference paths (mirror any edit into data/rooms.js)
  • assets/frames/ — add newer site photos, then list them in the room
  • assets/design-references/ — add or replace design images
  • styles.css — colours, typography, spacing
  • app.js — viewer behaviour, sections, ordering

Ask your AI assistant: “In gouna_walkthrough_sandbox, update <room / section / style> …”
