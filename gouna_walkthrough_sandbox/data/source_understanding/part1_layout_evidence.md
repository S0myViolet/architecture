## What Part 1 establishes
1. **Corner lot, unit 14A.** The walk starts on a brick-paved street at a corner;
   gate pillars are clad in rough dark stone and carry "14A". (00:00–00:08)
2. **Carport = timber pergola at the street corner**, beside (not on) the entry
   path. (00:09–00:11)
3. **Entry sequence**: street → stepping-stone path along the side wall (upper
   floor cantilevers over it, low stone boundary + planting outboard) → recessed

   (00:09–00:18)
4. **See-through living**: from the threshold the far glazing and white buildings

   a long corridor.
6. **Colonnade terrace**: thick round raw-concrete columns (gray, unfinished),
   irregular flagstone, wood-slat trim above the sliders, glass-railed terrace band
   above, low stone bench/counter at the garden edge. (00:29–00:40)
7. **Garden**: level sod lawn, low stone bench wall, block boundary topped with
   decorative breeze blocks, palms + white apartment blocks beyond. **No pool

8. **North façade, east end**: a second recessed slider (slat band + timber
   reveal) and a tall dark-gray rendered service-wall panel between glazing runs;
   a raised planter with flowering shrubs marks the corner where Part 2 picks up.
   (00:38–00:51)

- v4's stair sat behind the corridor's east end with a solid corridor mouth —
  from the living the stair could not be seen. Part 1 00:25 shows it clearly.
- v4's colonnade had 3 slim dark columns; video shows thicker raw-concrete
  columns and more of them along the full north face.
- v4 modeled a pool pit in the garden; Part 1's garden pans show level lawn only.
- v4 had no street, no unit-14A gate pillars, no second (east) recessed slider.
